---
name: blindspot
description: Use when the user wants to surface unknown unknowns, risks, or areas they have not considered. Turns blind spots into a prioritized exploration list.
---

1. Understand the current goal / codebase / plan.
2. Search for common failure modes, edge cases, and related concerns the user has not mentioned.
3. Present a short prioritized list of "known unknowns" the user can choose to explore further.
4. Do not expand into full research until the user picks items.
