# Product Management Skills

End-to-end product lifecycle framework for autonomous agents and founders.

## Phases
1. **Discovery**: Customer interview analysis, problem definition, competitive matrix, Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD).
2. **Strategy**: Positioning, monetization models, Moat analysis, and North Star metric definition.
3. **Specification**: Concise PRD authoring with user stories, acceptance criteria, non-goals, and edge cases.
4. **Execution**: Phased milestone breakdown, risk tracking, and sprint scoping.
5. **Launch & Growth**: Go-to-market checklists, release notes, landing page copy, and funnel metric telemetry.
